# NOTE: /note/* and /profile/* (and bare npub/nprofile landings) are served with
# a `noindex` robots meta tag instead of being disallowed here. That is
# deliberate: a Disallow rule would stop crawlers fetching those URLs, so they
# would never see the noindex and the pages already in the index would linger.
# Once they have dropped out of the index, adding an explicit Disallow here is
# the follow-up step that stops the crawl traffic itself.
